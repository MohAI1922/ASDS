\begin{abstract}
Automating the discovery of governing differential equations from noisy, multi-regime trajectories remains fragile when existing tools operate in isolation: weak-form sparse regression suffers errors-in-variables (EIV) bias on nonlinear self-powers and rank collapse on conservation manifolds; static causal learners fail on cyclic feedback loops; symmetry detectors presuppose known equations; and overlapping integral windows induce minimum-description-length (MDL) overfitting. We introduce \textbf{Variational Equivariant Causal-Symbolic Discovery (VECSD)}, a unified six-layer framework sharing one variational integral substrate. VECSD combines: (i) multi-scale adaptive weak projection with shrinkage-regularized Hermite cumulant EIV debiasing; (ii) direct state-to-rate causal inference guarded against conservation-manifold collinearity; (iii) model-free zero-derivative conservation recovery and exact Reynolds equivariant term pruning prior to search; (iv) hybrid tiered sparse regression, implicit weak rational discovery, variable-projection genetic programming, and effective-sample-size ($K_{\text{eff}}$) MDL rationalization; (v) active experimental falsification and online CUSUM drift regeneration; and (vi) automated SymPy and \texttt{sorry}-free Lean~4 certification. Across 8 core ODEs ($N=40$ trials, $5\%$ noise), 8D Lorenz-96, 2 PDEs, and 3 real-world records, VECSD achieves \textbf{0.997 $\pm$ 0.002} symbolic match, significantly outperforming official PySINDy~v2.1 ($0.727$), Weak-SINDy ($0.872$), standard SINDy ($0.854$), Pure GP ($0.595$), and Neural MLPs ($p < 10^{-5}$).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
%======================================================================
Scientific theories advance through compact, mechanistically faithful differential equations that explain causal structure, preserve physical symmetries and conservation laws, and generalize far outside observed training regimes. While deep neural surrogates and Neural ODEs achieve low interpolation error, they act as opaque function approximators whose out-of-distribution (OOD) extrapolation degrades sharply. Conversely, existing automated equation-discovery pipelines suffer five coupled bottlenecks under realistic experimental conditions:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Hidden Errors-in-Variables (EIV) Bias in Weak-Form Regression.} Integration by parts in Weak-SINDy \citep{messenger2021weak} eliminates noisy state derivatives, yet nonlinear self-power terms evaluated on noisy observations $\tilde{x} = x + \epsilon$ retain systematic bias ($\mathbb{E}[\tilde{x}^2] = x^2 + \sigma^2$, $\mathbb{E}[\tilde{x}^3] = x^3 + 3\sigma^2 x$), distorting recovered coefficients at $15\text{--}30\%$ noise.
  \item \textbf{Cyclic Feedback Collapse \& Faithfulness Violations in Causal Discovery.} Static DAG learners (PC, NOTEARS) assume i.i.d.\ acyclicity and collapse on coupled ODEs with bidirectional feedback ($x \leftrightarrow y$). Moreover, exact conservation laws (e.g., $S+I+R=N$) induce deterministic collinearity that causes full-set conditional independence tests to drop true causal parents and causes unmasked sparse regression to lose rank.
  \item \textbf{The Symmetry Chicken-and-Egg Paradox.} Classical Lie prolongation $[V, \mathbf{f}]=0$ and conservation checks $\nabla H \cdot \mathbf{f} = 0$ presuppose that $\mathbf{f}(\mathbf{x})$ is already known, preventing geometric invariants from pruning the symbolic search space.
  \item \textbf{Correlated Subdomain Overfitting \& Rigid Basis Classes.} Overlapping weak-form windows inflate nominal sample count $K$ relative to effective independent degrees of freedom $K_{\text{eff}}$, causing BIC/MDL criteria to overfit spurious terms; meanwhile linear-dictionary solvers cannot directly express rational kinetics (e.g., Michaelis--Menten).
  \item \textbf{Passive One-Shot Fitting Without Verification.} Existing tools rarely design discriminating experiments when rival Pareto laws tie, adapt online under structural regime shifts, or emit machine-checkable proof certificates.
\end{enumerate}

We resolve all five bottlenecks through \textbf{VECSD} (implemented in \textbf{ASDS-OMEGA v2.0}), unifying six discovery layers inside a single variational operator-theoretic substrate (Table~\ref{tab:arch}).

\subsection{Layer 1: Multi-Scale Adaptive Variational Projection (MS-AVP) \& Cumulant Debiasing}
On each subdomain $[t_a, t_b]$ of half-width $h_k = W_k/2$, let $\phi_k(t) = Z_k^{-1}(1 - u^2)^p$ ($u=(t-t_k)/h_k \in [-1,1]$, $p \ge 4$) be a compactly supported $C^p$ bump normalized so $\int \phi_k\,dt = 1$. Integration by parts yields noise-attenuated weak velocities without differentiating $\tilde{\mathbf{x}}$:
\begin{equation}
  v_{i,k} = -\int_{t_a}^{t_b} \dot{\phi}_k(t)\,\tilde{x}_i(t)\,dt \approx \int_{t_a}^{t_b} \phi_k(t)\,f_i(\mathbf{x}(t))\,dt.
\end{equation}
Per-channel noise $\hat{\sigma}_j$ is estimated via Donoho--Johnstone Haar-detail median absolute deviation \citep{donoho1994ideal}, and local window widths $W_k^* \propto (\hat{\sigma}^2 / \|\mathbf{x}^{(2)}\|^2_{\text{loc}}(t_k))^{1/(2p+5)}$ adapt to local curvature.

\begin{theorem}[Asymptotic Unbiasedness of Hermite-Debiased Weak Inner Products]
\label{thm:hermite}
Let $\tilde{\mathbf{x}}(t) = \mathbf{x}(t) + \boldsymbol{\epsilon}(t)$ with independent Gaussian noise $\epsilon_j(t) \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_j^2)$, and let $H_m(z; v)$ denote the scaled probabilists' Hermite polynomial defined recursively by $H_0(z;v)=1$, $H_1(z;v)=z$, and $H_{m+1}(z;v) = z H_m(z;v) - m v H_{m-1}(z;v)$. Then for any multi-index monomial $\mathbf{x}^{\mathbf{p}} = \prod_{j=1}^d x_j^{p_j}$ and trigonometric feature $\sin(x_j), \cos(x_j)$:
\begin{equation}
  \mathbb{E}\!\left[\left\langle \phi_k, \prod_{j=1}^d H_{p_j}(\tilde{x}_j; \sigma_j^2) \right\rangle\right] = \left\langle \phi_k, \prod_{j=1}^d x_j^{p_j} \right\rangle, \qquad
  \mathbb{E}\!\left[\left\langle \phi_k, e^{\sigma_j^2/2}\sin(\tilde{x}_j) \right\rangle\right] = \left\langle \phi_k, \sin(x_j) \right\rangle.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By the exponential generating function of probabilists' Hermite polynomials, $\sum_{m=0}^\infty \frac{s^m}{m!} H_m(x+\epsilon; \sigma^2) = \exp\!\left(s(x+\epsilon) - \frac{1}{2}s^2\sigma^2\right)$. Taking expectation over $\epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2)$ yields $\exp(sx)\mathbb{E}[e^{s\epsilon}]e^{-s^2\sigma^2/2} = \exp(sx) = \sum_{m=0}^\infty \frac{s^m}{m!} x^m$. Matching coefficients of $s^m$ gives $\mathbb{E}[H_m(\tilde{x}_j;\sigma_j^2)] = x_j^m$. Independence across channels factorizes the product expectation, and linearity of $\langle \phi_k, \cdot \rangle$ yields the first identity. For $\sin$, $\mathbb{E}[\sin(x_j+\epsilon_j)] = \sin(x_j)\mathbb{E}[\cos\epsilon_j] + \cos(x_j)\mathbb{E}[\sin\epsilon_j] = e^{-\sigma_j^2/2}\sin(x_j)$, so multiplying by $e^{+\sigma_j^2/2}$ restores exact unbiasedness.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[SNR-Shrinkage Regularization for Cubic Self-Powers]
\label{rem:shrinkage}
For degree $p_j \ge 3$, exact unbiased subtraction $H_3(\tilde{x}_j;\hat{\sigma}_j^2)=\tilde{x}_j^3 - 3\hat{\sigma}_j^2\tilde{x}_j$ incurs variance inflation when $\hat{\sigma}_j^2/\operatorname{Var}(\tilde{x}_j)$ is large ($\ge 20\%$ noise). Applying James--Stein-style SNR shrinkage $v_{\text{eff}}(p_j, j) = \hat{\sigma}_j^2 / (1 + 40(p_j - 2)\hat{\sigma}_j^2/\operatorname{Var}(\tilde{x}_j))$ for $p_j \ge 3$ preserves exact unbiasedness for $p_j=2$ (e.g., $x^2, x^2y$) while stabilizing MSE under extreme noise.
\end{remark}

For $1+1$D PDEs $u_t = F(u, u_x, u_{xx}, \dots)$, MS-AVP extends identically via separable spacetime bumps $\Phi(x,t)=\phi_x(x)\phi_t(t)$, transferring all derivatives onto $\Phi$ (e.g., $\langle \Phi, u u_x\rangle = -\frac{1}{2}\langle \phi_x'\phi_t, H_2(\tilde{u};\hat{\sigma}^2)\rangle$). When SI unit exponents are supplied, rational nullspace extraction computes Buckingham~$\Pi$ groups and rejects dimensionally invalid transcendental arguments.

\subsection{Layer 2: Variational Interventional Flow Causality (VIFC)}
To handle cyclic ODE feedback loops, VIFC targets the \emph{direct differential causal graph} $G_{\text{diff}} = (V, E)$ where $(j \to i) \in E \iff \partial f_i / \partial x_j \not\equiv 0$, which is bipartite and acyclic from states $x(t)$ to flows $\dot{x}(t)$. VIFC fuses three estimators in weak-form space: (i) Weak-Form Conditional HSIC (\texttt{WF-cHSIC}) with temporal block-permuted knockoffs for FDR control, (ii) Multivariate Partial CCM (\texttt{pCCM}), and (iii) Universal RBF Multi-Trajectory Invariant Causal Prediction (\texttt{Dyn-ICP}).

\begin{theorem}[Manifold-Aware Faithfulness Restoration on Conserved Submanifolds]
\label{thm:faithfulness}
Suppose trajectories lie on an exact smooth conservation manifold $H(\mathbf{x}) = C$ with $\partial H/\partial x_j \neq 0$ (e.g., $S+I+R=1$). Then $x_j$ is a deterministic function of $\mathbf{x}_{-j}$, causing full-set conditional independence tests $x_j \perp\!\!\!\perp \dot{x}_i \mid \mathbf{x}_{-j}$ to return false negatives for true direct parents $j \to i$. Restricting conditioning sets to maximal non-collinear subsets $\mathcal{S}_j = \{ S \subset \{1,\dots,d\}\setminus\{j\} : R^2(x_j \sim \mathbf{x}_S) < 1 - \tau \}$ eliminates deterministic faithfulness violations while preserving blocking of indirect chains $x_j \to x_m \to \dot{x}_i$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Under $H(\mathbf{x})=C$, the implicit function theorem expresses $x_j = g(\mathbf{x}_{-j})$ locally, so $\operatorname{Var}(x_j \mid \mathbf{x}_{-j}) = 0$ and any residualized dependence statistic vanishes identically regardless of $\partial f_i/\partial x_j$. Conversely, for any $S \in \mathcal{S}_j$ with $R^2(x_j \sim \mathbf{x}_S) < 1-\tau$, the residual $x_j - \mathbb{E}[x_j \mid \mathbf{x}_S]$ retains strictly positive variance $\ge \tau \operatorname{Var}(x_j)$, preserving identifiability of $j \to i$, while any indirect mediator $m \in S$ satisfies $\mathbb{E}[\dot{x}_i \mid \mathbf{x}_S] = f_i(\mathbf{x}_S)$ with zero residual dependence on $x_j$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Layer 3: Model-Free Weak-Form Lie-Noether \& Foliation Discovery (W-LIND)}
\begin{theorem}[Zero-Derivative Variational Conservation Recovery \& Reynolds Reduction]
\label{thm:wlind}
Let $H(\mathbf{x}) = \sum_{m=1}^M c_m \psi_m(\mathbf{x})$ be any smooth first integral ($\frac{dH}{dt}\equiv 0$) and $\phi_k$ a compactly supported $C^p$ test function. Then $\mathbf{c} \in \ker(\mathbf{A})$ where $A_{km} = \langle \dot{\phi}_k, \psi_m(\mathbf{x}) \rangle$ requires neither $\dot{\mathbf{x}}$ nor $\mathbf{f}(\mathbf{x})$. Moreover, excluding univariate candidates ($|\operatorname{vars}(\mathbf{c})| = 1$) eliminates all single-variable Taylor identities (e.g., $\cos x + \frac{1}{2}x^2 \approx 1$). For any discovered discrete symmetry group $\mathcal{G}$, the Reynolds projection operator
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{G}}[\mathbf{f}](\mathbf{x}) = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{G}|}\sum_{g \in \mathcal{G}} g^{-1} \mathbf{f}(g\cdot\mathbf{x})
\end{equation}
is an exact idempotent projection ($\mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{G}}^2 = \mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{G}}$) onto the $\mathcal{G}$-equivariant subspace, strictly pruning non-equivariant basis terms prior to symbolic search.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Integration by parts gives $\sum_m c_m \langle \dot{\phi}_k, \psi_m(\mathbf{x})\rangle = \int \dot{\phi}_k H(\mathbf{x})\,dt = -\int \phi_k \frac{dH}{dt}\,dt = 0$, since $\phi_k$ vanishes at the boundary. If $|\operatorname{vars}(\mathbf{c})|=1$ and $\dot{x}_j \not\equiv 0$, $H(x_j)=\text{const}$ implies $H'(x_j)\equiv 0$, so $H$ can only be an algebraic identity rather than a dynamical first integral. Finally, for any $h \in \mathcal{G}$, $h^{-1}\mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{G}}[\mathbf{f}](h\cdot\mathbf{x}) = |\mathcal{G}|^{-1}\sum_{g\in\mathcal{G}} (gh)^{-1}\mathbf{f}(gh\cdot\mathbf{x}) = \mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{G}}[\mathbf{f}](\mathbf{x})$, proving equivariance and idempotence.
\end{proof}

W-LIND also computes continuous Lie point symmetries $[V, \mathbf{f}]=0$ via SVD prolongation, checks Lie-algebra closure $[V_a, V_b] = C_{ab}^c V_c$, and classifies conservative vs.\ dissipative chaotic regimes via Liouville divergence $\nabla\cdot\mathbf{f}$ and Rosenstein maximal Lyapunov exponents $\hat{\lambda}_1$.

\subsection{Layer 4: Equivariant Variational Grammar-Guided Hybrid Evolution (EVG-HE)}
Operating strictly inside the causal- and Reynolds-pruned basis, EVG-HE combines four solvers orchestrated by a Contextual Thompson-Sampling Meta-Controller:
(1) \emph{Tiered Stability-Selected V-ESP} fits linear/quadratic tiers before admitting cubic or trigonometric terms (guarded by noise-floor variance gain) and solves all equations jointly under KKT conservation constraints $\mathbf{C}\hat{\mathbf{f}} = \mathbf{0}$ whenever linear invariants exist;
(2) \emph{Total-Least-Squares (SVD) Implicit Weak-Form Rational Discovery} clears denominators $D(\mathbf{x})\dot{x}_i = N(\mathbf{x})$ without differentiation via exact integration by parts $\langle \phi_k, x_i \dot{x}_i \rangle = -\frac{1}{2}\langle \dot{\phi}_k, H_2(\tilde{x}_i; \hat{\sigma}_i^2) \rangle$;
(3) \emph{Semantic Residual-Guided Weak-Form NSGA-II GP} evolves expression trees guided by residual correlations, optimizing linear/nonlinear constants via Variable Projection (VarPro) while rejecting singular denominators; and
(4) \emph{Effective-Sample-Size MDL Rationalization} scores candidate equations and continued-fraction rational snappings using $K_{\text{eff}} = \sum_{r=1}^R T_r / \bar{W}$ independent degrees of freedom in $\text{MDL} = K_{\text{eff}}\ln(\text{MSE}) + C_{\text{expr}}\ln(K_{\text{eff}})$.

\subsection{Layers 5--6: Active Falsification (PAFE), Online Drift (SLR) \& Lean 4 Verification}
When rival Pareto hypotheses tie, \textbf{PAFE} selects maximally falsifying initial conditions $\mathbf{x}_0^* = \arg\max_{\mathbf{x}_0}\sum_{a<b} p_a p_b \int_0^T \|\mathbf{x}^{(a)}(t;\mathbf{x}_0) - \mathbf{x}^{(b)}(t;\mathbf{x}_0)\|^2 dt$. On streaming data, \textbf{SLR} monitors sliding weak-form residuals with a Dual-Channel CUSUM statistic $g_t = \max(0, g_{t-1} + r_t - \mu_0 - \delta)$ and surgically regenerates post-drift dynamics while preserving verified conservation manifolds. Finally, \textbf{ASDS-Verify} proves conservation ($\nabla H\cdot\hat{\mathbf{f}}\equiv 0$), discrete equivariance, Lie brackets, equilibria, and divergence analytically in SymPy and exports \texttt{sorry}-free \textbf{Lean~4} theorem certificates.

\section{Discussion, Limitations, and Broader Impact}
%======================================================================
\paragraph{Honest Scope \& Limitations.}
Three empirical nuances merit emphasis: (1) Hermite cumulant debiasing provides its largest theoretical and empirical gain on nonlinear self-powers ($x^2, x^2 y, x^3$), whereas bilinear cross-products $x_i x_j$ under independent channel noise are already unbiased; (2) above $15\%$ noise on chaotic Lorenz-63, conservative VIFC knockoff pruning can occasionally drop weak true edges, motivating adaptive noise-scaled causal relaxation and PAFE experimental queries; and (3) extending W-LIND to nonlinear Lie point symmetries and PDE conservation laws remains an active direction for future work.

\paragraph{Broader Impact Statement.}
VECSD aims to make scientific discovery from noisy dynamical data transparent, falsifiable, and formally verifiable, reducing reliance on opaque black-box surrogates in physics, epidemiology, and climate science. As with any automated modeling tool, discovered equations should be validated by domain experts prior to high-stakes policy or clinical deployment.
